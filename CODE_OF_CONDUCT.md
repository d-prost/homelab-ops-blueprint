# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, specific, and technical.

Disagreement is expected. Personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disclosure of private infrastructure or credentials are not acceptable.

When reporting a problem, criticize the code or decision rather than the person. When receiving criticism, respond to the technical substance. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content that exposes sensitive data and may restrict participation when behavior repeatedly disrupts the project.

Security-sensitive reports should follow `SECURITY.md` instead of being posted publicly.
